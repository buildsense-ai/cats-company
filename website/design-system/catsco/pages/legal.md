# CatsCo Legal Pages

- Privacy and terms pages are pre-launch drafts, not final legal documents.
- Use readable single-column prose with a narrow measure and clear headings.
- State that formal text depends on the operating entity, jurisdictions, product data flows and legal review.
- Do not present unverified retention periods, subprocessors, certifications or statutory promises.
- Place a prominent draft-status notice before the dated update, and label unresolved topics as items for confirmation.
- Link to the contact explanation page only as an informational route; do not imply that the current form sends a legal request.
